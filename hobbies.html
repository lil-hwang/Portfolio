Interests

🎮

I enjoy competitive games and story-driven adventures. From intense ranked matches to immersive open worlds, gaming is one of my favorite ways to relax.

📖
Reading & Manga

I mostly read manga and shonen series like One Piece and Jujutsu Kaisen. I also enjoy books about self-improvement.

🎵
Music

My playlists are mostly hip-hop, punk rock, and alternative bands like Paramore, My Chemical Romance, and Linkin Park. Music is always playing while I study or lifting.

🎬
Film & Series

I enjoy thriller, anime, and sci-fi stories. Some of my favorites are Parasite, Old Boy and Project Hail Mary.

🚴
Cycling

I like going on casual rides during weekends to clear my mind and explore new places. It's a good balance between exercise and relaxation.

🎨
Arts

I enjoy digital art, manga panels, and creative design. I like exploring different art styles, especially anime-inspired illustrations and cinematic visuals.
